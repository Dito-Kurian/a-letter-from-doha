Dear Aloooo,

I honestly don't know where to start. I've never really written a letter like this before, so I hope you don't judge my writing too much. 😅

Your birthday was coming up, and I kept thinking about what I could do for you this time. I've been thinking about it for months, actually. Sometimes I had an idea, then changed my mind, and then came up with something else.

I still remember giving you chocolates last year. It was a small thing, but I wanted to give you something you could actually hold and enjoy. I wanted to do something like that again this year.

But well... I'm in Doha, and you're back in Kerala. I wish I could have given you something in person, but that wasn't possible this time.

I also thought about sending something by post, but it might take too long or reach your home directly. I didn't want a birthday surprise to create any unnecessary trouble for you.

So, after thinking about it for a while, I came up with this little idea.
